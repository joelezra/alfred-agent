# Admin Night App — TikTok & Launch Strategy

## The Launch Video

Cluely's playbook: **entertainment, not a demo.** Their skit got 13M+ views, 70K signups in week one.

### Our launch video concept

**Format**: Short film / POV skit (30-60 seconds)
**Tone**: Relatable comedy, NOT ragebait (Cluely got attention but had to backtrack)

**Concept**: "POV: You're the friend who actually got everyone to do their taxes"
- Open on a group chat: "admin night tonight?"
- Cut to: candles, wine, 4 friends on a couch, laptops open
- One person: "I just cancelled 3 subscriptions I forgot about"
- Another: "Wait I've been paying for Hulu twice??"
- Progress bars filling up on their phones
- End card: app name + "adulting is better together"

**Why this works**: It's the TikTok aesthetic people already recognize (admin night content), shows the product naturally, and the "Hulu twice" moment is the shareable hook.

**Production**: Doesn't need 6-figure budget like Cluely. This can be shot on iPhone — authenticity IS the brand. The cozy living room IS the set.

## Multi-Account TikTok Strategy

### Account 1: @[brand]tips — Educational Slideshows
- "5 bills you're probably overpaying"
- "The adulting checklist nobody gave you"
- "Things to do before you turn 26" (insurance deadline)
- "Tax season checklist for first-timers"
- **Format**: Carousels/slideshows (81% higher engagement than video, only 3% of TikTok content uses this format)
- **Goal**: Saves + shares (cognitive retention content)
- **Volume**: 3-5 posts/day

### Account 2: @[brand] — Lifestyle / Admin Night Vibes
- Friends doing admin night together — real footage
- "Get ready with me: admin night edition"
- Before/after: overwhelmed vs. progress bar complete
- Session recaps: "we cleared 23 tasks in 45 minutes"
- **Format**: Video (15-30 seconds)
- **Goal**: Brand awareness, FOMO, community
- **Volume**: 1-2 posts/day

### Account 3: AI UGC — Testimonial Machine
- AI-generated talking-head testimonials at scale
- "I found this app and now I actually do my admin"
- A/B test hooks, faces, scripts
- **Tools**: Arcads ($110/mo, 10 videos, most realistic) or HeyGen ($29/mo, unlimited)
- **Format**: UGC-style video (9-15 seconds for ads, 30-60 for organic)
- **Goal**: Conversion, paid ads feed
- **Volume**: 10-20 per week for testing, scale winners

### Account 4 (optional): Founder account
- Build-in-public content
- "I'm building an app for admin night — here's day 1"
- Revenue updates, user milestones
- **Goal**: Indie dev community, tech Twitter crossover

## Content Calendar Framework

### Week 1-4 (Pre-launch / Building Hype)
- Founder account: "building this" content
- Educational account: start posting checklists (no app mention yet, build audience)
- Tease the app in comments/bio only

### Week 5 (Launch Week)
- Launch video drops on main account
- All accounts post simultaneously
- Founder account: "we just launched"
- Educational account: "the app I've been hinting at"
- AI UGC: 20+ testimonial videos flood TikTok
- Cross-post to Instagram Reels, X, YouTube Shorts

### Week 6+ (Sustained Growth)
- 5-10 pieces of content/day across all accounts
- A/B test hooks weekly, double down on winners
- User-generated content reposts (people sharing their admin night sessions)
- Seasonal content: "tax season checklist," "back to school admin," "end of year review"

## The "Screenshot Moment" (Umax/Cal AI playbook)

Design one screen in the app that people WANT to screenshot and share:

**The session summary screen:**
```
Admin Night Complete! 🎉

You: 7/7 tasks ✅
Sarah: 5/6 tasks
Marcus: 4/5 tasks

Total: 16 tasks crushed in 45 min
Streak: 3 weeks 🔥

[Share to TikTok] [Share to Stories]
```

This IS the marketing. Every completed admin night = potential organic content.

**Social template for Instagram/Stories:**

The app generates a shareable card with:
- Group photo (taken at start of session or pulled from camera roll)
- Progress chart showing all participants' completion bars
- Total stats: tasks cleared, time spent, group streak
- App watermark/branding baked in

Think Spotify Wrapped but for adulting. After every session, one tap → beautiful card → Instagram story. The group photo makes it personal (friends tag each other = organic reach), the progress chart makes it brag-worthy, and the watermark drives installs.

This turns every admin night into a social post. Friends see it, ask "what app is this?", download, host their own admin night, post their own card. Infinite loop.
