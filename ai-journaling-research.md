# AI Journaling — Market Validation (Parked)

## Verdict: Crowded market, brutal economics, platform risk

### Key Numbers
- Day One: ~$6M ARR after 12 years (the ceiling)
- Rosebud: $6M funded, only 7,500 paying customers, ~$1.2M ARR
- Reflectly: 81.8K reviews, $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr
- Apple Journal: free on every iPhone, AI features coming
- Market: $5-6B, growing 10-18% CAGR

### Why Not (for now)
- Platform risk: Apple adds AI to Journal → value prop evaporates
- Competition: VC-funded teams (Rosebud, Mindsera) + 12-year incumbents (Day One)
- Retention: journaling apps see ~20% Day 1 retention, ~7-10% Day 30
- Margins: API costs for AI eat into $10-15/mo subscription

### Only Angle That Works
- Privacy-first on-device AI journaling (nobody owns this)
- But it's a feature, not a business
- Revisit when on-device LLMs are more capable (late 2026/2027)
