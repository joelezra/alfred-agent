# iOS App Store Market Research — March 25, 2026

Cross-platform synthesis from Reddit, TikTok, and X/Twitter research.

---

## Top Opportunities (ranked by signal strength)

### 1. Privacy-First Expense Tracker
- 60% of top 20 budgeting apps share user data with third parties (Incogni 2026)
- Users actively searching "budget app without bank login"
- YNAB at $14.99/mo creating price refugees
- Model: one-time purchase, offline-first, no account required
- MoneyWiz closest competitor but still imperfect
- **Moat**: privacy angle is a differentiator that justifies switching from free apps

### 2. Admin Night / Life Admin App
- Viral TikTok trend covered by CNN, Bloomberg
- No dedicated app exists — people use generic planners
- Social + gamified approach to bills, subscriptions, appointments
- Fits Greg Isenberg's pattern: "problem they repeat weekly + AI to make it work"
- **Risk**: trend may fade; needs to be useful beyond the trend

### 3. One-Time Purchase PDF Editor (iPad)
- MacRumors threads running for YEARS asking for this
- Adobe is subscription-only, alternatives are scammy or subscription
- Users will pay $15-25 upfront
- PDFgear is free but struggles with large docs
- **Risk**: competitive (PDF Expert, GoodReader, Foxit exist), but subscription fatigue creates opening

### 4. Vertical AI Utility (single-purpose)
- ChatGPT proved demand (770M downloads, +148% YoY)
- Users confused by general AI — want scoped tools
- Examples: AI meal planner, AI budget coach, AI journal
- Greg Isenberg: "200+ apps will hit $100k/month solving problems impossible until now"
- a16z: App Store releases surged 60% YoY from vibe coding
- **Risk**: Apple cracking down on vibe-coded apps; need genuine quality

### 5. ADHD Task Manager / Body Doubling
- Body doubling apps trending (Dubbii viral on TikTok)
- Generic habit apps don't account for executive dysfunction
- "Companion content" huge — users want presence, not productivity porn
- Underserved niche with passionate, paying audience
- **Risk**: small niche, but high willingness to pay

## Pricing — What Users Want

| Model | Sentiment |
|-------|-----------|
| One-time purchase ($5-25) | Strongly preferred |
| Freemium + one-time unlock | Acceptable |
| Low annual ($10-20/yr) | Tolerated if justified by real costs |
| Monthly subscription | Hated for utilities |
| $5+/month for utilities | Rejected — users seek alternatives |

**Winning formula**: Free core + $5-15 one-time unlock. No subscription. No account. Local-first with optional sync.

## Indie Dev Playbook (from successful devs)

1. Ship MVP same day idea forms (Claude Code + Cursor/Rork)
2. Design core interaction to fit a 10-second screen recording
3. Study top short-form videos in your category, write down the first 3 seconds
4. Record demos straight from simulator, post to TikTok as-is
5. Organic short-form > paid influencer marketing (Cravotta's approach)
6. Test hooks before touching code
7. Screenshot comments that explain the product — reuse that language
8. Portfolio strategy: compound small wins, learn faster (Max: 2 to 30 apps in 1 year)
9. Annual plans with heavy discounts create upfront cashflow
10. "2026 is the GREATEST time to build a startup in 30 years" — Greg Isenberg

## Underserved Categories (Tier 2 & 3)

### Tier 2 — Fragmented, no single good solution
- Meal planning + grocery (PlateJoy shut down July 2025, nothing fills gap)
- Vehicle maintenance tracking (users juggle 2+ apps)
- Home inventory/warranty tracking (people default to spreadsheets)
- Collection tracking (cards, vinyl, books — no universal app)
- Clipboard manager (iOS only stores 1 item; Paste is $2/mo subscription)
- Document scanning/OCR (good options exist but all subscription or cloud-required)
- Shortcuts/automation toolkit (Apple's Shortcuts is "rickety and limited")

### Tier 3 — Emerging/niche
- Aquarium management (hobbyists replacing notebooks)
- Post-surgery/disability fitness (generic fitness oversaturated, rehab underserved)
- Emotional well-being beyond meditation (growing faster than mindfulness)
- Activity-based dating (matching on shared activities)
- Offline file sharing ("still broken in 2025")
- Senior-focused services (underserved in hyperlocal)
