### YouTube Shorts Algorithm (2026 Updates)
- January 2026 update changed how Shorts are discovered and quality-filtered
- Key signals: watch time, swipe rate, engagement (likes, comments, shares), rewatchability
- No music = all revenue to creator pool; each music track = 33-50% goes to music licensing
- US/UK/Canada audiences = highest CPM; developing markets = lowest
- Niche matters: finance/B2B commands premium; entertainment earns less
- Q4 peaks; January typically lowest RPM month
