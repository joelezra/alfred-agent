### The 3-Stage LinkedIn Algorithm (2026)

**Phase 1: Bot Filter (0–15 minutes)**
- Screens for spam and engagement bait
- "Comment YES below" = actively suppressed
- Evaluates semantic depth for genuine professional value

**Phase 2: 60-Minute Small Audience Test**
- Post reaches only 2–5% of first-degree connections
- Strategically selected based on historical relevance
- Must achieve 5–10% engagement from this group within 60 min to advance
- First 150 characters before "See more" are critical — this is your hook

**Phase 3: Expanded Interest Graph Distribution**
- High performers graduate to 10–20% of network
- Then second/third-degree connections and beyond
- Algorithm identifies relevant professionals across entire platform — bypasses connection limits

### The Shift: Social Graph → Interest Graph
LinkedIn no longer shows content based on who you're connected to. It now shows based on:
- Hashtags and topics you follow
- Keywords you engage with
- Profile visit patterns
- Content dwell time
- Your posting history and topical authority signals

This means your posts can reach people outside your connections — if your content is topically consistent and gets strong early engagement.

### Wrong Approach vs. Right Approach

**WRONG (What Most People Do):**
- Chase viral content — "replicating 2023 viral success with same tactics completely failed in 2025"
- Focus on broad audience appeals ("I help entrepreneurs")
- Post for visibility and follower counts
- Spray cold connection requests and DMs
- Use engagement pods or artificial engagement
- Include external links in post copy
- Post generic motivational quotes
- Blindly repost without adding perspective
- Optimize for total impressions over relevant reach
- Measure outputs (likes, views) not inputs (consistency, quality)

**RIGHT (The Shift in 2026):**
- Treat LinkedIn as a positioning platform, not a content platform
- Target your right 500 people, not the wrong 5,000
- Build topic authority through niche consistency — same subject, week after week
- Optimize for "meaningful comments" (10+ words, substantive, adds perspective)
- Use strategic commenting on target audience's posts before/after your own content goes live
- Profile as conversion tool: specific positioning ("I help service-based businesses navigate the $500K–$1M Scalability Ceiling")
- Showcase specific case studies with metrics in Featured section
- Treat consistency as the primary metric — posting streaks > viral hope
- Content focuses on "zero-party data": unique insights, contrarian takes, first-hand experience
- Build pipeline, not an audience

### Content Format Performance (2026 Algorithm)

Best performing:
1. Document/PDF Carousels — highest organic reach, extended dwell time
2. Text-only posts with strong hooks — effective if first 2 sentences compel "see more"
3. Native video (under 90 seconds with captions) — 5x more engagement than static posts

Worst performing:
- External links in copy (lowest reach — LinkedIn wants users on-platform)
- Blind reposts without commentary
- Engagement bait phrases
- Off-niche posts outside established authority area

### Key Algorithmic Metrics (2026)

1. Meaningful Comments (15x weight) — 10+ word substantive comments > 10 likes
2. Dwell Time — time spent on post signals value; "See more" clicks are highly positive signals
3. Reposts with Insight — commenting when sharing beats blind shares
4. Topical Authority — posting history determines content category and distribution; educational niche content gets 3–5x more reach than personal updates
5. Initial Velocity — 20 comments in first 60 minutes > 50 comments over 24 hours

### The Authenticity Score
LinkedIn's 2026 algorithm introduced an Authenticity Score:
- Analyzes engagement patterns for artificial activity
- Detects engagement pods, automated comments, fake activity
- Profiles showing these patterns suffer dramatic reach penalties
- Genuine conversations prioritized for distribution

## Actionable Takeaways
1. Stop posting for visibility — define your "right 500 people" (your actual ICP) and optimize everything for them
2. Rewrite your headline to be specific: not "I help entrepreneurs" but "I help [specific type] achieve [specific outcome]"
3. Put your external links in comments, not in post copy — the algorithm actively suppresses posts with external links
4. Leave 10–20 substantive (10+ word) comments per day on your ICP's posts — this is the #1 inbound lever in 2026
5. Post 2–5 times per week consistently — consistency multiplied by niche authority beats viral once per month
6. Document/PDF carousel is the highest organic reach format right now — create one per week
7. Make your first 150 characters count — this is what users see before "See more" in the feed
8. Kill engagement pods immediately — LinkedIn's Authenticity Score detects and punishes them
9. Showcase case studies with specific metrics in your Featured section — it removes objections before the call
10. Measure your posting streak and topical consistency, not your impression count — those are the leading indicators
