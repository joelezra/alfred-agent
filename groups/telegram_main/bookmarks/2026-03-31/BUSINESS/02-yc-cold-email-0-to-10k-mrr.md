## Deep Dive
This is one of the most credible startup growth posts in this batch — Finn has receipts (YC backing, verifiable $2M raise, VentureBeat coverage), a prior agency background, and shared actual metrics.

**The 5-Step Playbook (from both tweet and blog):**

**Step 1: Ultra-specific ICP**
Company-level: 5-10 employees, B2B software, US-based, sales-led, $100k+ ACV deals, cold outbound-driven, recently founded. Persona: Founder. The key insight is ruthless specificity — "create such a perfect customer that if they heard about your solution they would have no choice but to say tell me more."

**Step 2: Build the list**
30-50 target companies found on LinkedIn. Decision-maker emails via Apollo or Wiza/Anymail Finder. Not mass lists — handpicked.

**Step 3: Write killer emails**
- 5-8 sentences (70%+ read on mobile)
- Break lines every 2 sentences (people skim)
- DO NOT talk about product features
- Talk about the person, their company, their pain points
- Reference your solution minimally

**Step 4: The call**
- 493 sales calls in first 3 months
- 90%+ of time understanding customer problems
- Offer full refund early-stage to reduce friction
- Don't pitch features; find the problem first

**Step 5: Close and iterate**
- "If we could deliver X, would you pay Y?"
- Refund guarantee removes early risk for customers

**What Origami built:** An AI agent platform that automates steps 1-3 of this process 24/7. Origami finds leads, enriches them from 100+ data sources, and runs the outreach. This is the meta-product — they dog-food their own cold email process to sell the tool that automates it.

**Verification:** VentureBeat confirmed the $2M raise and YC batch status. Product Hunt "Product of the Day" in Feb 2026. 1,000+ signups in February with no paid marketing. LinkedIn post from Finn confirms the metrics. High credibility.

**One skeptical note:** 5.3% reply rate is exceptional — industry average is 1-3%. Their ICP specificity and prior agency expertise likely explain this. Replicating this without that background may yield lower rates.

## Actionable Takeaways
- ICP specificity is the entire game — if targeting is wrong, nothing else matters
- 50-75 emails/day is the right volume for highly targeted outreach (not 500/day spam)
- Never pitch features; pitch the pain point and solution outcome
- Mobile-optimize all emails: 5-8 sentences, 2-sentence paragraphs max
- Offer refund guarantees to close first deals without friction
- Apollo and Wiza/Anymail Finder are the go-to email enrichment tools
- Once this works, automate it — Origami does this for you natively
