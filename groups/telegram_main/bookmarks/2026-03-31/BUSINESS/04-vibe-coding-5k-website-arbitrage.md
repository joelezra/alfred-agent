## TL;DR
Atenov describes a freelance arbitrage play: use Google Stitch (generates design from screenshot) + Claude Code (writes the code) + Vercel (deploys instantly) to build a working website prototype in ~1 hour, then charge $5,000 for it. Target: small businesses with outdated websites (medicine, real estate, car sales). The pitch is showing up with a working prototype instead of a proposal.

## Key Claims
- Full website in 1 hour, zero tool cost (all free tier tools)
- Workflow: Stitch screenshot → Claude code → Vercel deploy
- Target market: businesses with 2017-era websites that can afford to pay
- Pitch: show up with site already built, not a proposal — "they buy on emotion"
- Goal: 5-10 clients at $5,000 = $25k-$50k
- "Vibe coding is an arbitrage window. It's closing."

## Deep Dive
**The Workflow Verified:**
All three tools are real and the workflow is legitimate:

1. **Google Stitch** (stitch.withgoogle.com) — Google Labs AI design tool launched in 2026. Free (350 standard generations/month). Can take a screenshot of an existing website and generate a redesigned UI. Supports HTML/CSS, Tailwind, Vue, Angular, Flutter, SwiftUI export. March 2026 update added multi-screen generation, Voice Canvas, DESIGN.md, and interactive prototyping.

2. **Claude Code** — Anthropic's command-line coding agent. Can take Stitch's output and turn it into a full working site. Free tier available; API costs minimal for a single-page site.

3. **Vercel** — Free tier hosting with instant deploys. Industry standard for deploying static/Next.js sites.

**The Business Model Assessment:**
This is a genuine near-term arbitrage. The psychology is correct — showing a live working prototype collapses the buyer's decision from "should I hire someone?" to "should I use this specific site?" That's a much easier close.

The $5,000 price point is plausible for small business websites. Traditional web agencies charge $3,000-$15,000 for small business sites. The value is the speed and the prototype-first sales approach.

**The "closing window" claim is accurate.** As more people run this playbook, small businesses will become savvier and the novelty of a working prototype will wear off. This is a 2026 window, not a 2027 or 2028 window.

**Skeptical notes:**
- No case study or actual deal confirmation in the tweet — this is a framework, not a proven case study
- The crypto/Polymarket tagging in replies (@Polymarket, crypto accounts) suggests this may be cross-posted for crypto-adjacent engagement rather than pure business content
- Sustainability: this is a freelance play, not a scalable business. At 5-10 clients it produces $25k-$50k one-time, not recurring revenue
- One commenter (@arXS) notes "$5,000 for a website built in 1 hour is steep" — pricing pushback is valid in non-US markets
