## Deep Dive
**The Full 5-Channel System:**

**#1 UGC (User Generated Content)**
- Volume is the entire game: 30-60 videos/month per creator
- Most founders fail at UGC because they hire 1 creator and expect 3 videos to work
- Performance bonus structure: 100k views=$150, 250k=$300, 500k=$600, 1M=$800
- To find creators: scroll TikTok in your niche, DM manually, or hire a VA to send 100 DMs/day
- Contract required; text creators daily or videos never get made
- Tool: Layers.com for finding viral formats specific to your app (tinder-style interface showing formats that work in your niche, with AI replication)

**#2 Influencers**
- $1 CPM deal structure = $1,000 for 1M views
- Sell it as "$1,000 for 1M views" not "$1 CPM" (psychologically sounds better)
- Sweet spot: micro-influencers with 10k-50k followers but high engagement (above 10k avg views)
- 500k follower influencers will quote $5,000+; micro with crazy engagement are the gold
- Process: find on TikTok/IG → DM/email → pitch $1 CPM → negotiate → contract → brief → daily follow-up until posted
- Cap every deal at $1,000 total CPM

**#3 Faceless Content / AI Slop**
- "The people who say faceless content is dead are broke"
- Format types: AI slop videos (niche-targeted + ManyChat automation), character format (consistent persona teaching + selling), theme pages
- Winning formula: find ONE viral format → replicate endlessly with slight variations
- Example: breathing app doing "hold your breath challenge" 4x/week — same structure, different hooks
- Automatable inside Layers.com via slideshow generation

**#4 Founder-Led Content**
- Make content yourself to develop intuition for what works
- @cormachayden_ scaled his app to $300k/month doing this
- The push-scroll guys scaled to $100k/month with founder content → ran as paid ads
- Value: teaches you what hooks work, what makes people stop scrolling — feeds all other channels

**#5 Paid Ads (TikTok + Meta)**
- Best channel once CPA is cracked
- The flywheel: UGC/influencer/faceless → winning creative → paid ads → CPA data → onboarding optimization → more revenue → more ads
- Setup via Layers.com (2-3 clicks vs. traditional days-long process)
- Scale: 25% budget increase per week once winning creative confirmed

**The Flywheel:**
UGC → find hooks → brief influencers with same hooks → viral → put into paid ads → ads reveal converting messaging → update onboarding → better conversion → more budget → repeat

## Actionable Takeaways
- If building a B2C app: start with UGC before paid ads — find the winning format first

- The $1 CPM influencer deal structure is battle-tested and worth deploying immediately
- Find ONE viral format → replicate to death → that's the entire content strategy
- Text/message creators daily — without daily follow-up, content never gets made
- Layers.com is worth evaluating for viral format research and ad setup
- Build founder-led content first to develop the intuition before delegating to UGC
