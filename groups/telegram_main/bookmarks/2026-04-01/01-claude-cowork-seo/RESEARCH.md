## Deep Dive

### What Is Claude Cowork?

Claude Cowork launched in January 2026 as an autonomous AI agent that lives inside the Claude Desktop app. It is not a chatbot — it operates as an autonomous worker. You designate a folder, describe a task, and it executes independently: reading local files, browsing websites in Chrome, visiting pages, running multi-tab analysis, and delivering structured outputs like spreadsheets and reports, all running locally on your machine.

Key distinctions from Claude Chat:
- Direct access to local files (no upload/download cycle)
- Can open browsers and navigate real websites
- Handles multi-step workflows without human hand-holding
- Better for bulk or repeated operations across many pages
- Available on Pro ($20/month), Max ($100-200/month), Team, and Enterprise plans (Mac + Windows)

Interestingly, Claude Code itself generated Cowork's code in just 10 days — a notable dogfooding signal.

### The 20 Prompts (What's Publicly Known)

The full 20-prompt list is paywalled (via newsletter subscription), but from public research the system breaks down across six domains:

**Research & Competitive Intelligence (Prompts 1-3)**
- Competitive landscape mapping via Chrome automation
- Reverse-engineer competitor keyword strategies
- Content gap identification

**Keyword Strategy (Prompts 4-5)**
- Local services keyword workflow
- SaaS keyword workflow (separate prompt)

**Technical SEO Audits (Prompts 6-8)**
- Site health checks
- Schema validation
- Internal linking analysis

**Content Optimization (Prompts 9-11)**
- On-page scoring
- Pre-publish checklists
- E-E-A-T analysis

**Local SEO & Google Business Profile (Prompts 12-13)**
- GBP post generation
- 30-day content calendar creation

**Backlinks & Reporting (Prompts 14-15)**
- Competitor backlink reverse-engineering
- Monthly reporting framework

Bonus: A Claude Code extension with slash commands for AI search optimization (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT).

Example prompts from public posts:

Service Area Page Gap Analysis: "Open Chrome and crawl my website [URL] and these competitor sites: [URL1], [URL2], [URL3]. List every service+location landing page each site has."

Backlink Gap Analysis: "Open Chrome and go to Ahrefs. Run a backlink analysis on my domain [URL] and these 3 competitors. Pull total referring domains, domain rating, total backlinks with DR 40+, and which pages get the most links. Then find every site that links to at least 2 of my competitors but not me. Export everything into a spreadsheet."

### Hype Assessment

The claim that this replaces a "$10K/month SEO agency" deserves scrutiny:

- True parts: Claude Cowork can genuinely automate research, competitive analysis, and content auditing tasks that used to require expensive tools (Ahrefs, SEMrush) or agency labor
- Sarvesh acknowledges: "the strategy still requires a human brain — knowing which keywords bring in revenue, understanding your local market, and reading competitor data. Claude does the research; you make the decisions that matter"
- The "$10K saved" framing is marketing, not literal — you're not replacing a full agency. You're replacing the research and execution labor that agencies charge for. The strategic judgment stays with you.
- For a blue collar business owner who currently does zero SEO, even a fraction of this output would be transformative
- For someone who already pays a real agency, the comparison is more complicated (agencies bring relationships, link-building networks, accountability)

Bottom line: The capability is real. The ROI framing is aggressive marketing from a genuine expert. For a local business owner at $0 SEO spend, this is legitimately powerful.
