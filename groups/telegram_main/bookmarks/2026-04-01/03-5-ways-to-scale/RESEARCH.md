### The 5 Scaling Methods (Reconstructed from Public Threads)

Based on Ernesto's documented strategy across multiple threads, the "5 ways" are almost certainly a structured version of these core techniques:

**1. Creator/Influencer Partnerships ($0 Upfront)**
The cornerstone of his strategy. Instead of paying for ads or creating content himself, he contacts micro-to-mid-tier influencers in his app's niche and proposes a revenue split or free app access deal — no upfront cash required. His VA sends 100 DMs/day at $5/day labor cost. The conversion math: even at 1-2% response rate from 100 DMs, you get 1-2 influencers per day testing your app. Each influencer brings their audience directly into the funnel.

**2. TikTok/IG Reels Content Engine**
He documents case studies of apps generating 100M+ views via TikTok slideshow accounts posting 3x/day, averaging 36K views per post with outliers hitting 20M+. The content is not product demos — it's value-first content in the app's niche that funnels to the app via link-in-bio. He built a tool (tryviewtrack) to track video view analytics across creators who post in his app's category.

**3. AI Marketing Agent ("Eddie")**
He built an OpenClaw agent named "Eddie" to automate parts of the marketing workflow for his $70K/month operation. The Eddie thread is one of his most viral (from around March 2026). The agent handles: TikTok content research, performance tracking, creator outreach prioritization, and campaign iteration. This connects directly to the startupideaspod research (bookmark 5) about the "Larry Loop" AI marketing agent.

**4. Rapid 7-Day Validation Before Building**
His pre-build checklist: (a) Are there influencers in this niche? (b) Is there a large audience? (c) Are there communities (Reddit, Discord, Facebook groups)? (d) Are there course creators? (e) Are there existing competitors (validates demand)? If all 5 are yes, build. If not, skip. This directly prevents building apps nobody wants.

**5. Subscription Pricing + Review Velocity**
He runs annual subscription pricing ($109.99/year is his cited sweet spot) and prioritizes getting 1,000+ reviews early. One app got 1,000 reviews in 38 days. Review velocity feeds App Store algorithm placement, which feeds organic installs, which compounds. He tracks this with his own tool (AppsScale).

## Actionable Takeaways

1. Before building any consumer app, run his 5-point niche validation: influencers? large audience? communities? course creators? existing competitors? All 5 = proceed.
2. The $0 upfront influencer deal structure is directly replicable: hire a VA, send 100 DMs/day to influencers in your app's niche, offer revenue share or free access, close deals without upfront cash
3. Annual subscription pricing ($109.99/year) over monthly — reduces churn, improves LTV, improves App Store placement signals
4. "Build marketing first, then the app" — figure out the exact TikTok content niche before writing code
5. Track creator video performance (tryviewtrack pattern) to know which influencers drive installs before doubling down on them
6. The 7-day build cycle (ChatGPT + Cursor + Xcode) means you can test 4 apps per month — fail fast, find the one that works
