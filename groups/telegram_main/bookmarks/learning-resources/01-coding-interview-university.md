- Graphs

**Algorithms**

**System Design** (for senior candidates)

**Core CS**
- Big-O notation and complexity analysis
- Caching, processes/threads, OS fundamentals
- Networking basics
- Compilers (light treatment)

**Scope:** The creator notes: "There is a lot to learn in a university CS program, but only knowing about 75% is good enough for an interview."

## Time Commitment

The plan is designed for months of study at 8-12 hours per day. For someone with existing engineering experience working part-time:
- Realistic timeline: 6-12 months at 2-3 hours per day
- Accelerated with AI tutoring: can shorten by 30-40%

## Honest Assessment in 2026

The irony of bookmarking this: in 2026, with AI pair programming, the question is whether traditional DS&A interview prep remains the right path.

**Still relevant for:**
- FAANG and FAANG-adjacent companies that still run algorithm interviews
- Building genuine CS fundamentals that make you a better engineer
- Roles in systems/infrastructure where CS depth matters

**Less relevant for:**
- Most product companies that have moved to practical/project-based interviews
- AI-assisted development roles where speed of iteration > algorithm optimisation

The repo is still the best free resource for anyone targeting the traditional FAANG interview process.
