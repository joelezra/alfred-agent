## Context: Why LinkedIn Strategy Matters in 2026

LinkedIn is experiencing a renaissance in 2026 for a specific reason: **organic reach is still surprisingly high** compared to X, Instagram, and TikTok, because most creators underuse it. The algorithm still rewards quality content, especially for B2B niches.

For someone building SaaS or consulting:
- B2B decision makers are on LinkedIn in ways they're not on other platforms
- The "being wrong" frame usually refers to using it as a résumé/job board instead of as a content distribution channel

## Common "Wrong" LinkedIn Patterns

Based on what typically goes viral in LinkedIn strategy content:

**Wrong way:**
- Only post when job hunting
- Only connect with people you know
- Share articles without commentary
- Treat it as a digital CV
- Post polished corporate-speak

**Right way:**
- Consistent content creation (2-3x per week minimum)
- Personal insights > shared articles
- Vulnerability and "here's what I learned" framing
- Engagement in comments of big creators in your niche
- Using LinkedIn's Creator Mode features (newsletter, live, etc.)
- Direct outreach with value-first approach (not "let me sell you something")
