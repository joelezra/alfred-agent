# Market Research: Analytics Tools for Indie Hackers & Founders
**Date:** 2026-03-28
**Target:** Indie hackers, micro-SaaS founders, solo startup founders, online store owners
**Incumbents:** GA4 (free/confusing), Mixpanel ($140-2289+/mo), Amplitude ($22k-250k/yr), Hotjar ($39-99/mo)

## The Core Problem
GA4 is technically free but practically broken for solo founders:
- **48-hour data delay** — you can't see what happened yesterday until tomorrow
- **58% of tech users block Google Analytics** (Plausible's own research) — you're missing your core audience
- **75%+ of SEOs rated themselves "not happy" with GA4** (MarketingProfs survey)
- Basic task (find traffic sources) required a **1,400-word tutorial** — Glenn Gabe, documented
- UA historical data deleted January 1, 2024 — lost years of data overnight
- GDPR violations ruled in 7 EU countries — legal risk

## What Solo Founders Actually Check Daily (4 things, nothing else)
1. Visitor count
2. Top traffic sources
3. Top pages
4. Signups/conversions

That's it. Everything else is noise for a solo founder.

GA4 buries this, delays it 48 hours, and misses 15-58% of tech visitors entirely.

## User Persona: "Marcus"
- Indie hacker, 1-2 products, $0-$5K MRR
- Checks analytics first thing every morning (5 min max)
- Wants: visitor count, top sources, signups — one screen, real-time
- Pays: $9-19/mo is the sweet spot
- Discovers tools through: Hacker News, Indie Hackers community, Twitter BIP (build in public), Reddit r/indiehackers
- HATES: GA4 setup complexity, cookie banners, missing data from ad blockers

## The Perfect $9-19/mo Tool (doesn't fully exist yet)
One-screen dashboard combining:
- **Cookieless web analytics** (no GDPR issues, no ad-blocker blind spots)
- **Stripe MRR integration** (see revenue alongside traffic)
- **Simple funnel** (visitor → trial → paid)
- **5-minute setup** (drop a script tag, done)
- **Real-time data** (not 48-hour delay)
- **Daily email digest** (top metrics in your inbox every morning)

PostHog has pieces of this but is moving upmarket. Plausible does analytics but no revenue integration. Nobody has combined all three simply.
