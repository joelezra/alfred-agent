# Aisle: distribution study
Checked revenue (Jul→Aug→Sep→Oct 2026): $270 → $3,726 → $9,855 → $24,819 · Destination-wedding website builder (RSVPs, guest list, guests book and pay for rooms) plus a venue/vendor marketplace (US, Stripe) · Free to build; $19.99/mo to publish (7-day trial, card up front), 5% on guest payments, 10% venue commission on bookings

## Main channel(s)
1. **Programmatic and content SEO, plus AI-assistant distribution (ChatGPT app, Claude directory, MCP).** Confidence: **medium**. About 10,400 indexable URLs: 7,193 venue pages, 2,833 vendor pages, 146 venue hubs, 83 guides, free tools, 5 destination budget calculators. Venue pages were bulk-refreshed Sep–Oct, the months revenue took off.
2. **Founder story and short-form social (TikTok/Instagram/Pinterest) around the founders' own Italian castle wedding.** Confidence: **low-medium**. The TikTok has a high like-to-follower ratio, but the account is small and no viral post was verified.

## Evidence
- Public counters on the homepage: 3,099 weddings planned, 68 countries, 33,063 guest names, 9,428 venues. https://aisle.wedding/
- Build-in-public "Notes": 3 weddings in April (founders "emailing venues one at a time"), 578 on 2 Aug, 1,130 on 23 Aug ("225 in the last seven days"), about 2,400 on 18 Oct ("over 300 in the last week"). https://aisle.wedding/notes/one-thousand-weddings , https://aisle.wedding/notes/october-2026
- Sitemap: 10 sub-sitemaps, 10,395 URLs. Venue sitemaps: about 4,600 lastmod Sep 2026 and about 2,570 Oct 2026. Guides: 82 of 83 lastmod Oct 2026. Vendors: 2,022 lastmod Jul 2026. https://aisle.wedding/sitemap.xml
- Long-tail templates include /venues/country/italy, /vendors/photographers, /guides/destinations/zanzibar-wedding-guide, /tools/budget-calculator/tulum, /wedding-dress-codes/black-tie, and free no-email templates (checklist PDF/CSV, budget spreadsheet, vows).
- Comparison SEO: /compare and /compare/aisle-vs-aisle-planner (to separate the brand from the established "Aisle Planner").
- AI distribution: a homepage banner reads "Aisle now works in ChatGPT". There is a ChatGPT plugin link, a Claude directory listing, and an MCP server with 17 public tools usable without sign-in (venue search, budget estimate, invitation wording…). It is also listed on mcpservers.org and mcpbundles. https://aisle.wedding/mcp , https://claude.ai/directory/aisle
- robots.txt explicitly allows Googlebot, Bingbot and GoogleOther and blocks faceted filter URLs (?country=, ?price=…), so they manage crawl budget deliberately. https://aisle.wedding/robots.txt
- Wikidata entity (Q140819239) and Organization/Person schema with founder sameAs links: entity SEO for Google and LLMs.
- TikTok @aisle.wedding: 1,217 followers, 68 videos, **30.5k likes** (~25 likes per follower). https://www.tiktok.com/@aisle.wedding . Instagram @aisle.weddings and Pinterest @aislewedding are linked (counts not retrieved).
- Founder story is front and center: Perrie & Walt married at Castel Fragsburg (Merano) in June 2026, and their site is the live example and the 12 "showcase" templates. Co-founder Walt Lafky: X @LafkyWalt, LinkedIn, IG.
- Supply side: free venue listing, 10% commission on Aisle bookings, "venues that answer come first". https://aisle.wedding/venue-pitch . Pages also exist for planners and vendors.
- Built-in virality: every couple's site is seen by 50–100+ guests (RSVP in "two taps, no app"), plus "Ask the group" venue voting.
- Reddit and press: no organic mentions found by web search. No tracking pixels or UTM parameters in the homepage HTML.
- Revenue mix: MRR is only $2,459 from 123 subscriptions, while October revenue was $24.8k with single days of $3,070 and $3,568. *Inference:* most revenue is guest room payments and venue booking flow through Stripe, not subscriptions.

## Growth story
- Mar–Jul: founders' own wedding (June) used as the demo; manual venue outreach; tiny revenue (June's $2.5k probably includes their own room-block payments; inference).
- Aug: gallery, plus-ones, registry and dress-code pages shipped. Weddings went 578 → 1,130 in three weeks.
- Sep–Oct: about 7k venue pages refreshed, 82 guides published, ChatGPT/Claude launch. Weddings reached about 2,400–3,100.
- *Inference:* revenue grows faster than wedding count because 2027 weddings are now entering the room-booking and payment stage (GMV lag), and US engagement-season traffic (Oct–Feb) is starting.

## Playbook to copy
1. Use your own life event as the product demo and origin story (real photos, a real live site, a public counter).
2. Scrape or build a directory of the supply side (venues/vendors) as thousands of SEO pages with location hubs. Block faceted URLs in robots.txt.
3. Ship free calculators and templates with no email gate, and one page per destination for each tool.
4. Expose the product as an MCP / ChatGPT app with useful no-login tools so LLMs recommend it.
5. Take a % of the money that flows through you (guest payments, bookings) on top of a small subscription.
