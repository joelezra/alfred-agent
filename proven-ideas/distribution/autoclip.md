# Autoclip: distribution study
Checked revenue (Jul→Aug→Sep→Oct 2026): $7,269 → $7,643 → $10,068 → $12,410 · AI tool that turns YouTube/Twitch/Kick videos into captioned 9:16 clips and auto-posts them · Free tier; Starter $19.99, Pro $39.99 (3-day trial), Scale $79.99/mo (via Whop)

## Main channel(s)
1. **Positioning inside the Whop "clipper economy", plus Whop distribution.** Confidence: medium. The pitch is "built for clippers, not creators". Clippers are people paid per view through Whop Content Rewards and Vyro campaigns. AutoClip connects to those campaigns, bills through Whop, has Whop's global affiliate program switched on, and its Whop store lists 389 members (against 373 active subs on TrustMRR). It's sold as a money-making tool, not an editing tool.
2. **Programmatic and AI SEO at scale.** Confidence: medium. The site has about 850 URLs, and about 10–18 new blog posts go up every day. Search results for clipper queries are dominated by autoclip.dev pages. Domain rating is only 4, so it's long-tail traffic, but 11.5k visitors in 30 days at $1.01 revenue per visitor is meaningful.
The founder listed **TikTok** as the channel, but the brand account is small (see Evidence). TikTok is probably where clippers post *outputs* (watermarked clips on Free and Starter plans) more than it's a direct ad channel.

## Growth story
- $520 in Mar, $900 in May, $3,678 in Jun, $7,269 in Jul. Inference: the June/July jump lines up with the Whop billing and campaign integration (late Mar) maturing, and with Pro going watermark-free (Jun 17), which made the trial more appealing.
- Clipping campaigns boomed on Whop in 2026, and clippers chasing CPM payouts want volume automation (channel monitoring plus auto-post to many accounts).
- Aug was flat. Sep and Oct grew about 30% and 23%. Inference: this matches the SEO publishing burst that started around Sep 19 compounding, plus word of mouth in clipper communities (Discord/Whop).
- Revenue per user rises as clippers stack plans or move to Scale for 25 social accounts.

## Playbook to copy
1. Sell to people who **make money** with your tool (the "clipper" or bounty economy). Show their earnings, not your features, and connect to their payout platform.
2. Bill through the marketplace where your buyers already live (Whop) and switch on its global affiliate program.
3. Publish programmatic comparison, alternative and "how to X for Y" pages daily, aimed at each sub-niche of your user (/for/bounty-clippers, /for/twitch-clip-channels).
4. Watermark free output so every free user's posts advertise you on TikTok.
5. Run a high-volume, dog-fooded brand account that posts your own tool's output.
