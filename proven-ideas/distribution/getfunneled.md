# Get Funneled: distribution study
Checked revenue (Jul→Aug→Sep→Oct 2026): $9,775 → $8,018 → $15,393 → $20,146 · Post-click landing pages and funnels (advertorials, listicles, quizzes, pre-sell pages) for Shopify D2C brands running paid ads, sold as SaaS plus a done-for-you service · Starter $349/mo, Growth $699/mo, Done-for-you $1,450/mo ($145 per landing page), Performance custom

## Main channel(s)
1. **Founder-led demo sales, with "build a funnel for your store live on the call" as the hook.** Every pricing tier sends visitors to "Book a demo" (cal.com/jameshamelsmith/30min). There is no self-serve checkout. Confidence: **medium-high**.
2. **An Australian performance-marketing agency partner network.** Agencies refer brands, earn lifetime recurring commission and supply the case studies. Confidence: **medium**. The structure is clearly there, but how many deals partners bring is unverified.

## Evidence
- Pricing has no self-serve path. Every plan button says "Book a demo", and the done-for-you plan at $1,450/mo is labeled "Popular" (https://getfunneled.com/pricing/). TrustMRR lists pricing as "1450p/m USD", with 21–22 active subscriptions and MRR of $18–18.8k, about $850 per account.
- Partner directory: 11 certified agencies, almost all in Australia. Platinum partners are Blue Sense Digital and DataSauce & Sticki (Melbourne). Gold partners are Ecom Nation, Elephant Room, Swan Studio and Zebra3. Silver partners are Blitz Growth, Buttr, Growth Huntr, Webprofits and Swanky (https://getfunneled.com/partners/directory/). The partner program pays "recurring commission ... for the life of the account" (https://getfunneled.com/partnerships/).
- A separate affiliate program targets newsletters and communities, with recurring commission and no minimums (https://getfunneled.com/affiliates/).
- Case studies come from the partner channel. DataSauce "doubled MER in 6 weeks", DataSauce ran Origani's Meta ads (-38% spend, +60% purchases), and Savvy Touch saw CPA fall from $38 to $15. Most are dated Mar 17–31, 2026 (https://getfunneled.com/customers/).
- SEO is a minor channel. Ahrefs domain rating is 4 (TrustMRR). The sitemap has about 115 URLs: 13 "vs/alternatives" pages (Replo, PageFly, GemPages, Funnelish, ClickFunnels, even "vs Claude"), about 30 integration pages and only 1 blog post (dated 2026-10-01) (https://getfunneled.com/sitemap.xml).
- Shopify App Store: the "Funneled" app (a checkout-link tool) launched May 14, 2026 and has 0 reviews (https://apps.shopify.com/get-funneled). It is not a driver yet. The developer address is Moonee Ponds, VIC.
- Social footprint is tiny. LinkedIn has 167 followers and 3 employees (https://www.linkedin.com/company/funneled), and the founder has 136 X followers. The founder is shown as "James Claridge" on TrustMRR. The demo calendar belongs to "James Hamel-Smith", whose role was not verified.
- The company is hiring a "Founding GTM Lead (Americas)" to "build pipeline, close ecommerce brands" (homepage). Outbound is planned, not yet built.
- Charge sizes in October include repeated $769 and $384. These match $699 and $349 plus 10% Australian GST, which suggests many customers are Australian (TrustMRR daily data, inference).
- The business is listed for sale ("partner's pregnant") on TrustMRR.

## Growth story
- The business is lumpy and B2B. MRR moves in steps of single $700–$1,500 accounts, so a few closed deals explain the jumps.
- *Inference:* the July–August dip (-18%) looks like churn or paused done-for-you accounts in the Australian winter, a slower period for many apparel and beauty brands. September–October growth (+92% versus August) fits closed demos and partner referrals before Black Friday / Cyber Monday, when D2C brands build pre-sell and bundle funnels for Q4 ad spend. That seasonal timing is the strongest explanation.
- The October 21 charge of $4,730 suggests an annual or multi-month prepay (an annual Growth plan is about $7.2k, so this may be partial or quarterly).

## Playbook to copy
1. Sell through agencies that already hold the client budgets. Give them lifetime recurring commission, a tiered directory listing (Platinum/Gold/Silver) and co-branded case studies.
2. Make the demo itself the product proof ("we'll build your funnel live on the call"), and put every tier behind a call so pricing can stay high ($349–$1,450/mo).
3. Add a done-for-you tier priced per deliverable ($145 per page). It lifts revenue per account, and AI keeps the margin around 95%.
4. Write "vs competitor" pages early. They are cheap and capture buyers who are already comparing tools, even at domain rating 4.
5. Start in one dense, connected agency scene (Melbourne/Sydney D2C) before hiring for the US.
