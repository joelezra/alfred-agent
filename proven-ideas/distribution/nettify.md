# NETTIFY: distribution study
Checked revenue (Jul→Aug→Sep→Oct 2026): $1,222 → $3,312 → $2,727 → $5,863 · Norwegian AI website builder with .no domain, hosting, booking, done-for-you sites, webshops and an SEO service · Free; Nettsidebygger 399 kr/mo (3,000 kr/yr); Levert done-for-you 800 kr/mo; Webshop 1,999 kr setup + 499 kr/mo; SEO 3,200–5,000 kr/mo (prices ex VAT)

## Main channel(s)
1. **Norwegian-language commercial-intent SEO pages.** These are about 53 "nettside-<trade>" landing pages, about 73 guides, "X-alternativ" pages and free tools, plus llms.txt to court AI search. Confidence: **medium**.
2. **Google Ads, with Meta (Facebook/Instagram) retargeting.** The tracking tags are installed and the founder lists both. Confidence: **medium-low**, because spend and creatives could not be seen.

## Evidence
- The founder lists "SEO, Facebook, Instagram, Google Ads" as marketing channels, and Ahrefs domain rating is 41. The site is for sale. The founder's note says "The bottleneck was distribution in the small Norwegian market". The founder is Kevin Nguyen, founded January 2026 (https://trustmrr.com/startup/nettify).
- Sitemap: 200 URLs (https://nettify.no/sitemap-0.xml). They include:
  - 53 trade pages: nettside-frisor, -rorlegger, -elektriker, -tannlege, -advokat, -snekker and more.
  - 73 /guide/ posts, such as "hva koster en nettside", "beste nettsidebygger norge 2026" and "lage nettside med chatgpt".
  - Alternative pages for Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, WordPress and Shopify.
  - Commercial keyword pages: lage-nettside, billig-nettside, gratis-nettside, nettside-pris, nettside-priskalkulator.
  - 27 English mirror pages.
- Lastmod dates: 26 URLs in June, 29 in July, 19 in August, 8 in September and 118 in October 2026. The pages are actively expanded and refreshed.
- Free-tool lead magnets: domain search, WHOIS, company-name generator (checks Brønnøysund), SEO check, speed test, mobile test, accessibility (UU) check and meta-tag check (sitemap).
- robots.txt explicitly allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and others, and the site publishes /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt. This is a deliberate play for visibility in AI answers (https://nettify.no/robots.txt).
- The homepage carries a Google Ads conversion tag (AW-17950227676), GTM-KT77ZWPB and a Meta pixel (fbq). Paid acquisition and retargeting are set up (https://nettify.no/).
- Social links point to facebook.com/nettify.no, instagram.com/nettify.no and LinkedIn nettifyno. Follower counts were not retrieved. The founder has 0 X followers.
- Proof assets: "500+ nettsider laget" (homepage), 25+ delivered projects with live URLs (https://nettify.no/kundehistorier/), "5.0 based on 29 reviews", and an SEO results page with real Search Console numbers for client sites. One example is lysefjordenrental.no with 822 clicks and 7.6× growth (https://nettify.no/seo-resultater/).
- Revenue mix (inference): 57 active subscriptions at $2,040 MRR is about $36 per subscription, close to 399 kr. Most subscribers are on the self-serve builder. One-off spikes such as Oct 29 ($1,317) fit setup fees or annual and SEO plans.
- Subscriptions grew from 15 when the listing was updated in June 2026 (search snippet of the TrustMRR listing) to 57 in November.

## Growth story
- *Inference:* the step-up from about $2k/month to $5.9k in October came after the SEO buildout (trade and guide pages, refreshed heavily in October) and the move upmarket to done-for-you sites, webshops and a 3,200 kr/mo SEO retainer. Higher-ticket one-off sales on top of a small base make the curve jumpy (Aug $3.3k, Sep $2.7k).
- *Inference:* the July low ($1.2k) is the Norwegian "fellesferie" holiday, when much of the country stops work. August and October bounce back as small businesses return to work.
- Google Ads probably fills the gap while the new pages age. Domain rating 41 is strong for a site that is about 1 year old, so expect some backlink or PR work.

## Playbook to copy
1. Choose a small-language market where global builders such as Wix and Squarespace do weak local SEO. Own "website for <trade>" pages in that language, one per profession.
2. Make free utility tools around the purchase moment (domain check, business-name check against the national registry, SEO and speed checks).
3. Ladder pricing from DIY (about $36/mo) to done-for-you (800 kr/mo) to an SEO retainer (about $290/mo), so the same traffic brings more revenue.
4. Publish real proof: live client URLs and actual Search Console screenshots instead of vague testimonials.
5. Open robots.txt to AI crawlers and ship llms.txt, so ChatGPT and Perplexity cite you for "best website builder in <country>".
