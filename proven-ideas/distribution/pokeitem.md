# PokéItem: distribution study
Checked revenue (Jul→Aug→Sep→Oct 2026): $6,974 → $9,464 → $20,385 → $32,332 · French app that scans Pokémon / One Piece / Riftbound / Yu-Gi-Oh! / DBZ cards and tracks collection value like a portfolio (RevenueCat, iOS + Android + web) · Free; Premium €5.99/mo, Premium+ €49.99/yr, Lifetime €159.99

## Main channel(s)
1. **App Store search in France (category ranking plus a French-language moat).** Confidence: **medium-high**. 468 of its ~490 iOS ratings are in the French store. It ranks #11 top-grossing and #13 top-free in FR "Reference", alongside competitors PokéCardex (#3) and iEstims (#8). It is pitched as "the French app with French/Cardmarket prices" against US-priced competitors.
2. **Gamified referral, UGC and creator loop built into the app (quests, XP leaderboard with real Pokémon prizes, "Clipping" XP per view, 20% creator affiliate program).** Confidence: **medium**. Season 1 of the contest started on 1 Sep 2026, the month revenue more than doubled. The brand's own social accounts are small, so reach has to come from users and creators rather than the brand.

## Growth story
- *Inference:* May–Aug ($2.7k → $8.8k → $7.0k → $9.5k) was early adopters from the founders' X/crypto audience and Discord, plus the FR App Store.
- The **September jump (+115%)** lines up with: v1.2.0 social, marketplace and messaging in August; One Piece TCG plus quests in v1.2.1; and the **contest season starting on 1 Sep** with heavy referral XP. Then the Pokédex/holo update (28 Sep), Riftbound for Premium+ (30 Sep) and back-to-school collecting season.
- *Inference:* October (+59%) adds compounding chart position (FR Reference top 15), new-set content SEO (Méga-Évolution / "Règne Delta" set pages), and creators. Daily revenue spiked on 11–13 Oct ($1.5k–2.3k/day) with no public cause found.
- Expanding to 5 TCGs and 9 card languages widens the paying base without new channels.

## Playbook to copy
1. Pick a hobby where the incumbent apps price in USD/English. Launch as "the [country] version" with local marketplace prices (Cardmarket here) and rank in a small App Store category.
2. Run a seasonal in-app XP leaderboard with **physical prizes the niche craves** (about €1k/season of sealed boxes). Weight referrals heavily and gate them on real activity (≥10 items, 5 login days).
3. Pay UGC in XP per view ("Clipping") rather than cash. Require a tag and a disclosure, and review every submission by hand.
4. Run a 20% lifetime-recurring affiliate program for micro-creators with no follower floor, plus a public "our creators" page.
5. Publish an SEO page for every product drop ("most expensive cards in set X", "which box to buy").
