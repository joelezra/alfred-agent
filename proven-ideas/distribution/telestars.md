# Telestars: distribution study
Checked revenue (Jul→Aug→Sep→Oct 2026): $5,058 → $13,160 → $27,739 → $28,010 · AI chatbot + CRM that lets adult/OF-style creators and agencies sell locked media for Telegram Stars inside DMs · Free (20% AI-sales fee / 7% manual) · Starter $29.90/bot/mo (15%/5%) · Pro $59.90/bot/mo (10%/2.5%)

## Main channel(s)
1. **Affiliate/referral network inside the OnlyFans-management (OFM) scene**: managers, "traffic" sellers and creator-tool review sites push `app.telestars.io/signup?ref=` links. Confidence: **medium**.
2. **Direct B2B sales to OFM agencies over Telegram** (founder DM + Calendly demo), with a take rate on sales. Revenue swings with a few large accounts. Confidence: **medium-low** (this is inferred from revenue shape; nobody says it outright).
Organic social, Product Hunt-style launches and paid ads show almost no footprint.

## Evidence
- Most revenue is sales fees, not subscriptions. TrustMRR shows current MRR **$1,885 from 40 active subs** against **$27.6k revenue in the last 30 days**, so about 93% is take rate on creators' Stars sales (https://trustmrr.com/startup/telestars).
- Daily revenue is lumpy. Baseline is about $250–600/day, with spikes of **$2,889 (Oct 6), $4,550 (Oct 13), $3,697 (Oct 18), $2,745 (Oct 24), $3,795 (Oct 28)**. That pattern fits periodic fee settlements from a handful of big agencies (inference) (TrustMRR profile).
- Affiliate evidence: a Google Sites "OnlyFans automation software" blog by model "Rundalya" ranks Telestars #1 vs SirenDM and CrmChat. It links `app.telestars.io/signup?ref=1ad7dcb1` throughout and points readers to its own Telegram channels (t.me/alonedrops, t.me/alikopen). It says "managers Alon Manager and Serenity" brought it up in a chat (https://sites.google.com/view/onlyfansautomationsoftware/telestars-new-2026-trend, …/sirendm-vs-telestars-vs-crmchat-review).
- Pricing changed. That affiliate post quotes Starter $19.90 / Pro $39.90 with **no free plan**. The site now shows a **Free plan with a 20% AI fee** and Starter $29.90 / Pro $59.90 (https://telestars.io/). Inference: the switch to a free tier plus usage fee likely widened the top of the funnel.
- The founder sells through Telegram himself: the site's contact is t.me/keitagram ("Keita｜telestars.io"), plus t.me/telestarsinfo_bot and a Calendly demo link (calendly.com/telestarsupp/telestars-demo). Homepage CTAs are "Book a demo call" and "Contact us now on Telegram".
- Positioning is a funnel argument aimed at OF/Fanvue/Fansly/Patreon creators who get traffic from IG, TikTok and dating apps: "bio link → Telegram chat → unlock with Stars" vs a 6-step OnlyFans checkout. "Trusted by 1,000+ creators and agencies". The homepage counter claims $5M generated, which doesn't fit $87k all-time fees at a 10–20% take; treat it as marketing.
- SEO is new and small. Sitemap has **136 URLs**: 20 English blog posts (Feb–May 2026, e.g. /blog/telegram-onlyfans, /blog/onlyfans-chatbot) and **112 programmatic "compare" pages** (16 competitors such as Infloww, Substy, Supercreator, OnlyMonster, CrmChat × 7 languages: en/fr/es/de/it/ro/tr). **118 of the 136 have lastmod Oct 2026**, so they're too recent to explain the Aug–Sep jump. Domain rating 7 (https://telestars.io/sitemap.xml).
- "TeleFans" is a fan-facing storefront and listed as "TeleFans acquisition" in every plan. It's pitched as a discovery and acquisition layer for creators (https://telestars.io/blog/telegram-onlyfans). A 2023 French WordPress site, telefans.app, sells a similar Telegram monetization bot. I couldn't confirm the two are linked.
- Weak launch footprint: PeerPush listing Feb 7 2026 with 7 upvotes by @kebranbaban (https://peerpush.com/p/telestars). X followers 0. No pixels (Meta/TikTok/Google Ads) in the homepage HTML.

## Growth story
- Jan–Jun 2026: $0.5–3.7k/mo while the product found fit with small creators.
- Jul→Sep (+450%): *inference*. One or more large OFM agencies probably onboarded many models/bots and routed their Stars sales through the AI. Supporting signs are the big recurring settlement spikes, the tiny sub count, and fee-heavy revenue. The free plan + 20% AI fee likely boosted sign-ups through affiliates in the same window.
- Oct flat ($28.0k): agency volume steady, MRR down 4.5%. Growth depends on a few whales rather than broad self-serve adoption (inference).
- Tailwind: OnlyFans bans AI chatbots in DMs (Clubic report), which pushes AI-chatting agencies toward Telegram, where Stars payments need no ID checks.

## Playbook to copy
1. Charge a **take rate on revenue you generate** (free plan + % of AI-closed sales). Agencies adopt with no risk, and revenue scales with their volume, not seat count.
2. Recruit the **gatekeepers** of a niche (agency managers, "traffic" sellers, tool-review bloggers) with ref links. In grey-market niches they reach buyers that ads and SEO can't.
3. Sell where the buyers live. Here that's a founder Telegram handle + demo-call CTA instead of email/forms.
4. Pitch a **funnel-friction argument** ("6 steps vs 2") that buyers can check against their own conversion data.
5. Once you have traction, ship **programmatic competitor-comparison pages × languages** (16 rivals × 7 langs = 112 pages) to catch switchers.
