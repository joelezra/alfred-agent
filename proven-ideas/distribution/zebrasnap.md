# ZebraSnap: distribution study
Checked revenue (Jul→Aug→Sep→Oct 2026): $18,587 → $25,839 → $23,584 → $38,640 · AI marketplace where sports-event photographers sell race photos that athletes find by selfie, bib number or shirt color · Free for photographers with 13% commission per sale; Lite $16.99/mo (10%), Pro $29.99/mo (6%), Enterprise $99.99/mo (4%), plus Stripe fees

## Main channel(s)
1. **Supply-side recruiting of sports photographers in Latin America, especially Costa Rica, Mexico and Puerto Rico.** Each photographer then brings their own buyers: they post album links to their Instagram and WhatsApp audiences after every race. Instagram (the founder's listed channel) works mostly through the photographers' accounts. ZebraSnap's own accounts are small. Confidence: **medium-high**.
2. **Photographer-to-photographer referrals and community spread**, using credits that waive the commission. Confidence: **medium**. The program exists, but its share of growth is unknown.

SEO (event and calendar pages) is new and probably not yet a revenue driver. Confidence that it matters today: **low**.

## Evidence
- The sitemap has 8,821 URLs (https://www.zebrasnap.com/sitemap.xml). Of these, 7,217 are `/events/` pages, 388 are `/calendar/<country>/<region>` pages, about 1,044 are real albums under `/en/<photographer>/<album>`, 8 are blog posts and 42 are help pages. 7,498 of the URLs have a lastmod of October 2026.
- Most `/events/` pages are placeholder pages for upcoming races, filled from a race calendar. For example, Titan Trail Run (South Africa) shows "0 photos", has an athlete "Get notified" alert and a "Covering this event? Publish your album here" call to photographers. The registration link "Opens running.life" (https://zebrasnap.com/events/titan-trail-run). This is programmatic SEO that collects demand on both sides before any photos exist.
- Real supply is concentrated. 88 photographer handles have albums in the sitemap, and the top 5 have 207, 98, 74, 68 and 52 albums. Most are Costa Rican (.cr, "Tico", Claro 5G race), Puerto Rican or Mexican. On Nov 1 the homepage listed Claro 5G (Costa Rica, 8,605 photos) and Carrera ESPN Runaholics (Mexico City, 7,695 photos).
- Album pages carry the photographers' own calls to action, such as "tag me on Instagram @cromanfoto" and "write to me on WhatsApp 8711-0888". The buyers belong to the photographers (https://zebrasnap.com/en/cesarromanfoto/carrera-claro-5g, https://zebrasnap.com/en/RiveraVisuales/fondo-lindora-9).
- Photographer handles match Instagram-style usernames. The product ships an "Instagram Stories in seconds" feature (shipped July 5, 2026) for announcing albums (https://zebrasnap.com/whats-new).
- Referral program: an invited photographer gets $25 credit and the inviter gets $50 credit after the invitee's first sale. Credit waives ZebraSnap's commission. Shipped May 16, 2026 (https://zebrasnap.com/help/rewards-and-referrals).
- The product is built for Latin America. The Mexican Stripe platform accepts OXXO cash and SPEI payments, dLocal Go covers local methods, and "direct payments" for countries without Stripe such as Venezuela shipped Oct 30 (https://zebrasnap.com/help/commissions-and-fees).
- The founder is Alfonso, a Mexico City developer and runner. His blog post (Nov 22, 2025) positions ZebraSnap as a modern "alternative to LumePic / PhotoHawk" (https://alfonsobries.vercel.app/posts/zebrasnap-the-simple-way-to-buy-sell-race-photos).
- Owned social is tiny. The YouTube channel @zebrasnaphq has 5 subscribers and 209 videos. TikTok @zebrasnap showed `followerCount: 0`. Instagram has two accounts, @zebrasnap_ and @zebrasnap_global, but their counts could not be read.
- Daily revenue spikes on weekends: Oct 10–12 $1.7–2.8k per day, Oct 24–26 up to $3.7k, Nov 1 $5.2k. Midweek days fall to about $100–300 (TrustMRR). Sales follow race days.
- The for-photographers page claims "+1.4M photos uploaded, +2.5M faces detected, up to $58.6K earned by a photographer per month" (https://zebrasnap.com/for-photographers).

## Growth story
- The business is driven by events. Each photographer who switches from LumePic or Instagram brings every race they shoot, and buyers convert right after the race (weekend spikes).
- *Inference:* the August jump and the record October follow the Latin American race calendar. August includes the Mexico City Marathon season. October/November has dense 5K/10K weekends, with multi-photographer coverage of one race (Claro 5G had at least 4 photographers). September dipped because it had fewer or rained-out events.
- *Inference:* October also stacked product and pricing pushes. Commissions dropped and storage doubled (Oct 20), a 14-day trial on paid plans arrived, "notify me when my photos appear" alerts shipped (Oct 26), Dutch, Polish and Czech locales were added, and the 7k-page event calendar went into the sitemap.
- Revenue compounds month over month (Dec 2025 $2.4k → Oct 2026 $38.6k). This fits a supply flywheel: more photographers per race give buyers more reasons to search, and more photographers join through referrals.

## Playbook to copy
1. Win the supply side, and let suppliers bring the demand. Recruit the creators who already have local audiences (photographers, coaches, tutors) and give them tools for promoting their links (Instagram Stories generator, branded galleries).
2. Start in one dense geography (here Costa Rica/Mexico) with local payment methods (OXXO, SPEI, dLocal) that incumbents ignore.
3. Pay referrals in fee waivers (credit against commission), not cash, so they cost little and reward active sellers.
4. Build programmatic "upcoming event" pages from a public calendar, with both a buyer alert and a supplier call to action, so search and email demand exists before any supply does.
5. Ship small features weekly and publish them on a changelog. It shows momentum to the suppliers you are recruiting.

## Unknowns
- Instagram follower counts, and whether the founder or team does direct outreach to photographers by DM.
- Whether TrustMRR revenue is ZebraSnap's commission only or includes gross photo sales (Stripe Connect setup). This changes how big the market is.
- Whether the event and calendar pages are indexed or bring traffic. Google Search Console is not connected.
- The share of US revenue versus Latin American revenue. The TrustMRR country is "US", but the album supply looks mostly Latin American.
- No Reddit, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt or paid-ads evidence was found. Reddit blocked the queries, and the Meta Ad Library returned a 403.
